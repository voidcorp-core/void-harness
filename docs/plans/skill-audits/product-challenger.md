# Specialist audit: `product-challenger`

## Need and boundary

Product-shaped missions need an independent challenge before solution work. This role owns user
outcome, evidence, smallest valuable scope, assumptions, non-goals, and measurable acceptance. It
does not invent strategy or own design, architecture, implementation, or prioritization.

## Sources and adaptation

YC user-research guidance and the harness brainstorm product pressure-test were distilled into a
bounded evidence check. Conversational coaching, generic ideation, roadmap ownership, and solution
generation were rejected. Routing is limited to the `product` predicate.

## Overlap audit

Experience Designer consumes validated intent to shape interaction. Solution Architect evaluates
technical structure. Neither reopens product value, keeping responsibility overlap below 30%.
